In Finland, employers must report every wage payment to the Incomes Register, maintained by the
Finnish Tax Administration. Two reports are required:

- **Earnings payment report**: one per payment, reported within five days of the payment date.
- **Employer's separate report**: once a month, even when no wages were paid.

Reports can be submitted through the e-service, the technical interface or on paper.

This module uses the technical interface's **Web Service deferred** channel.
